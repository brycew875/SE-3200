Yá'át'ééh! Welcome to Learning Navajo

Welcome to my webpage! I am a student in SE 3200 interested in web development and language learning. My main focus this semester is building simple digital tools to help people practice and learn Navajo (Diné Bizaad).

App Ideas for This Semester

  • Navajo Audio Dictionary: A site where users can click a word to hear audio pronunciations.
  • Flashcard App: A study tool for memorizing Navajo vocabulary.
  • Daily Phrase Widget: An app that displays a new Navajo phrase every day.
